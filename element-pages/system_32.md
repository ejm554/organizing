## System 32

- 32 mm cabinetmaking system
- Popularized by IKEA
- Characteristic are the columns of 5-millimetre (0.20 in) holes with centres at 32-millimetre (1.3 in) intervals.
- Wikipedia page: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/32_mm_cabinetmaking_system
    - "... a set of principles that has evolved for the production of ready-to-assemble and European-style, frameless construction custom cabinets and other furniture. While not defined by any standardization body, it is in wide use globally..."
    - "System 32 enables reconfigurable placement and spacing of shelves, doors, drawers and hardware. Most significantly, it simplifies and harmonizes dimensions, production processes and products for fitting, machine and furniture manufacturers, enabling efficiency and cost reduction."
- Related: KISS II, "A simplified approach" (see [PDF](https://www.woodweb.com/knowledge_base_images/zp/kiss_2_system.pdf))
