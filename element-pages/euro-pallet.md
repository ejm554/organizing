## Euro-pallet

- Compatible with Euro container
- Comes in fractional sizes
   - Standard size ("EUR"/"ISO1"): 800 mm × 1,200 mm × 144 mm
   - Half size ("EUR 6"/"ISO0"): 800 mm × 600 mm × 144 mm
   - Quarter size: 600 mm × 400 mm
   - Eighth: 400 mm × 300 mm
